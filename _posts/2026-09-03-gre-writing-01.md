Reading through an example essay that received the score of 5.0, the evaluation seems quite tolerant to logical flaws.

## topic
Science and technology will one day be able to solve all of society's problems.

## my thoughts
- problems are defined only under a certain morality.
- morality is considered independent from objective truth, regardless of its existence.
- controlling the morality of somebody else for their own good is therefore cannot be justified.
- there exists at least one pair of community where morality collides for a certain decision making.
- therefore, science and technology cannot deterministically solve all of society's problems; solving a problem will create another follow-up problems.

## real-world examples
- gene editing (CRISPR)
- AI automation
