Conflict of interest refers to a situation where an agreement between two parties results in a loss for one of them.

Think of buyers and sellers fighting over prices, or nations in warfare fighting over destructive power. Reconciling these interests is notoriously difficult, leading to massive resource expenditure with uncertain returns.

I propose a fundamental solution: **open communication powered by collective intelligence.**

### The Dilemma and Open Communication

Consider the classic Prisoner’s Dilemma. Theoretically, both parties can achieve a mutually beneficial outcome by maximizing their collective gain rather than selfishly competing. 

So why does this fail in real life? 

### What is Missing in Real Life?

In traditional models, parties operate in isolation, driven by mistrust and asymmetric information. What is missing in real life is a transparent system that fosters continuous, open dialogue. 

By leveraging collective intelligence—where information is shared freely and decisions are optimized for the whole group—we can shift the paradigm from a zero-sum game to a win-win dynamic. Open communication bridges the gap between individual self-interest and mutual benefit.
